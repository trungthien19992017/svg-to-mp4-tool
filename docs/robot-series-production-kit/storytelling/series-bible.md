# Robot Series Bible — v1.0

## Canon and approval
Two recurring robots: male and female. The exact master SVG bytes are locked in `masters/characters/`. The personalities below are **provisional editorial readings**, not approved character canon.

## On-screen identity
Male: cream body, dark face visor and turquoise facial features. Female: turquoise body/hood, pale face and pink accents. Preserve proportions and visual language from the masters.

## Emotional storytelling
Episode 01 uses a quiet rainy-room meeting, reaction, approach and hand contact. Favor anticipation, pauses and clear reactions over arbitrary bouncing. Do not use subtitles as a substitute for missing animation.

## Limitations
The Phase03 dynamic motion catalog marks actions Partial. V4 expression approval is still pending. Consequently automatic generation must not silently treat these as Supported. The Golden Sample demonstrates one authored episode, **not universal action support**.

## Reuse strategy
Reuse masters, existing rainy-room layers, episode audio with license review, and the validated composition. New actions require explicit artwork/motion QA and a new kit version; never mutate locked SVG masters in place.
