SIX INDEPENDENT VECTOR VIEWS

Open comparison-overlay.html in a browser. Fully offline; all comparison images are embedded.
Six SVG files are path-only vectors on transparent backgrounds, without embedded raster, linked assets or front-view transformations.
Each SVG traces its corresponding source view. Original sheet proportions and visible occlusion are preserved.
Paper texture is simplified to a compact flat-color vector palette; gradients and paper grain are simplified to flat colors. Paths are not semantic body parts and are not rigged.
Source crops are included for inspection. manifest.json records crop coordinates and path counts.
